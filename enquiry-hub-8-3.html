ENQUIRY HUB

ABOUT THE PROJECT

This is Enquiry Hub, where we maintain logs of all forms of inquiries. These may include academic inquiries as well as inquiries from other areas.

Anyone can view the inquiry logs. However, we only provide the inquiries themselves. We do not necessarily refine, edit, or modify an inquiry before storing it here.

Our current architecture allows users to submit inquiries indirectly through the provided options.

We do not publicly provide information about who submitted an inquiry. We may store the source of an inquiry in our internal logs.

Our team may classify inquiries according to the subject they belong to. If an inquiry cannot be clearly assigned to a subject, we may place it in an Unclassified folder.

If we recognize that an inquiry belongs to multiple subjects, we may place it in a Semi-Mixed Subjects folder.

For broad or overlapping subjects that do not fit cleanly into an existing classification, we may also place the inquiry in the Unclassified folder.

We may not provide solutions, explanations, answers, or any additional context for the inquiries.

We store inquiries in only two formats: internal logs and external logs.

Inquiries are stored as tokens.

We maintain only a textual database.

We are a business unit of Zon Institute.
